# Hello

I'm Devin, a Ph.D Candidate in High Energy Particle Physics at Texas A&M University. I work on the Compact Muon Solenoid Experiment (CMS) at the CERN Large Hadron Collider (LHC) where we try to do fundamental science and answer the universes most basic questions. I get to work with mountains of data and I am deeply interested in data science and data visualization. 

## Currently

- Working on physics analysis searching for a new particle decaying into Higgs bosons, X to HH to bbWW, with Run 3 CMS data.
- Improving the Cathode Strip Chamber's (CSC's) Level-1 Trigger for HL-LHC and Run 3 CMS data.
