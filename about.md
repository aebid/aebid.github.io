# About

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in High Energy Particle Physics at Texas A&M University. I work on the CMS experiment at the Large Hadron Collider. After 4 years at CERN I am back at Texas A&M to finish my degree.

Most of my work is spent finalizing a physics analysis where we are searching for a heavy particle decaying into a pair of Higgs bosons, X → HH → bbWW, with CMS Run 3 collision data. Outside of the analysis I also mentor other Ph.D. students and oversee general work on the CMS CSC Level 1 trigger system.

My primary interest is data analysis and visualization. I strongly believe that data tries to tell a story and most confusion is in the method of presentation. Taking the complex messy data from physics experiments at the LHC and turning it into an easy to understand plot is exciting.

When I am not at work I can be found snowboarding, hiking, crocheting, practicing instruments, or solving puzzles.
